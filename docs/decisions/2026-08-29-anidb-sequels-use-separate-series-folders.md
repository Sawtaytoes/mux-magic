# 2026-08-29 — Store AniDB sequels in separate series folders

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date decided:** 2026-08-29
- **Area:** naming
- **Source:** Owner chat, 2026-08-29

## Decision

Each distinct AniDB anime entry has its own library folder, even when its episodes retain a shared franchise season number. Fire Force Season 2 uses `Fire Force (2020) [anidb-15335]` and keeps its `s02eNN` filenames.

## What we rejected — DO NOT revert to this

Do not put a sequel AniDB entry into the preceding show's folder merely because the release or Plex numbering calls it the next season. Do not move the preceding show's specials into the sequel folder as part of this correction.

## Why it must not be re-litigated

AniDB treats the sequel as a separate show with its own specials and assets. The owner explicitly corrected the initial placement: "for anidb, this is typically something that goes into its own shows folder with its own specials etc."
